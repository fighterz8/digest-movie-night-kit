DIGEST MOVIE NIGHT KIT
Your own Netflix of free, public-domain movies.

SET IT UP (about 15 minutes)
0. Windows only: Docker Desktop needs a Windows feature called WSL. Open the Start menu,
   type PowerShell, right-click Windows PowerShell and choose "Run as administrator", then type
       wsl --install --no-distribution
   and press Enter. When it finishes, restart your PC. (Skip this on a Mac or Linux.)
1. Install Docker Desktop (free for personal use): https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/
   Open it once and wait until it says "Engine running". You can skip the sign-in.
   If it says "Virtualization support not detected" on Windows, do step 0 first.
2. Put this folder somewhere with plenty of free space. Not inside OneDrive or iCloud,
   or your movies will try to upload.
3. Open settings.txt, pick a password, and save it.
4. Double-click start:
     Windows: start.bat
     Mac:     start.command  (the first time, right-click it and choose Open)
     Linux:   run ./start.sh
   The first start takes a few minutes. When it says READY, two pages open in your browser.
   If Windows asks whether Docker may use your network, choose Allow (your TV needs it).

ADD A MOVIE
In Radarr: click Add New, type the title, pick the right YEAR (remakes aren't free),
make sure "Start search" is ticked, and click Add Movie. That's it.

WATCH
On this computer: Jellyfin opens in your browser.
On your TV or phone: install the free Jellyfin app, type the address start showed you,
and sign in with your password.

EVERYDAY
Your movies are in media\movies.
To stop everything, double-click stop. To bring it back, double-click start.

IS THIS LEGAL?
This kit only searches the Internet Archive's feature film collection, and only films
marked public domain. Public domain rules depend on your country; these films are public
domain in the US. Downloading movies you don't have the rights to is illegal in most places.
